Analysis treats every joint as a pin: members carry axial load only, and seat, tank, pegs, shock and engine load the nodes. Welded joints, lateral loads and cornering are not modelled, and the engine is treated as a dead weight on E and F: its leftover shake (about 1.1 tonnes-force at 15,000 rpm, mostly at twice engine speed) is not in these load cases, so use rubber or test the frame before riding. A frame that carries a rider needs real FEA and physical testing first. Forces are in newtons, tension positive.
