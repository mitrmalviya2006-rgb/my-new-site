Analysis treats every joint as a pin: members carry axial load only, and seat, tank, pegs, shock and engine load the nodes. Welded joints, lateral loads and cornering are not modelled, and the engine mounts are placeholders. A frame that carries a rider needs real FEA and physical testing first. Forces are in newtons, tension positive.
